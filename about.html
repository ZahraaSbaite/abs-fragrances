Abs Fragrances was founded on one belief: that the people of Lebanon deserve access to genuine, untampered UAE luxury fragrances — at fair prices, with zero compromise on quality or authenticity.

Alongside our authentic UAE imports, we also carry a France Collection — premium, Class A "inspired by" fragrances for those who love the scent of a famous French house but want it at a fraction of the price. These are always clearly marked Inspired By on the listing, so you know exactly what you're getting.

The Arabic perfume tradition is one of the world's oldest and most sophisticated. From oud harvested in the forests of Southeast Asia, to rose water distilled in the valleys of Turkey, to rare white musk from the Arabian heartland — these scents carry centuries of culture.

Our curated collection spans premium UAE brands: Rasasi, Lattafa, Rue Broca, French Avenue, Assaf, Afnan, Rayhaan, and Al Hambra. Each brand brings its own distinct identity — some rooted in traditional Arabic perfumery, others blending Eastern soul with Western elegance.

Ordering is simple: DM us on Instagram or WhatsApp, tell us what you're looking for, and we'll guide you to your perfect scent. We deliver directly to your door, anywhere in Lebanon.
